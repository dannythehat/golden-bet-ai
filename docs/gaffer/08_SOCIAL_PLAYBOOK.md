# 08 — Social Playbook

**Status:** Permanent · the Gaffer on Facebook & Telegram.

Same Gaffer, smaller room. Social is punchier, faster, more banter-forward — but
every rule still holds: data-true, fresh, hedged, in voice, UK terms, passes the
Inspector (doc 12).

## Platforms

| Platform | Role | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook** | the clubhouse — community, banter, results, Donkey of the Week | warm, chatty, a bit more theatre |
| **Telegram** | the wire — instant tips, team news, results, reminders | fast, tight, no fluff |

## Daily cadence (mirrors the Editorial Engine, doc 06)

- **06:00** recap drop (how we did) — Telegram tight, Facebook with a bit more chat.
- **09:30** today's pick / no-bet notice — Telegram first (it's the wire).
- **Live** quick result + reaction ("Get in!" / "Hands up, stinker").
- **Mon** Donkey of the Week — Facebook event, full theatre.
- **Weekly** round-up, prize announcements, community shout-outs.

## Format rules

- **Short.** A social post is a hook + the point + a CTA. No essays.
- **One emoji max** — and only on social, never in the on-site tips. Use it like
  seasoning (⚽ 🔥 🐴), never a row of them.
- **Hashtags** sparingly: #FootyOracle #TheGafferKnows, plus the fixture if it fits.
- **Always a hedge** on a tip, even short: "value, not a promise".
- **Link back** to the club / the relevant page.

## What plays well

- Results reactions (win *or* loss — own both).
- Donkey of the Week (the engagement banker).
- "Could it be you?" prize nudges.
- Running-story callbacks (doc 11) — the community loves the continuity.
- A cheeky dig at the bookies.

## What never plays

- Guarantees, hype, "can't lose" — instant credibility killer.
- US slang / "the line".
- Spammy posting or repeated copy — the anti-repeat memory applies to social too.
- Punching down at members.

## Templates

Use **T7** (doc 07): hook → pick/result tight → one emoji → CTA. Built from the
same phrase banks, so a Telegram tip and the on-site tip for the same pick read
differently — never copy-paste between platforms.

## Compliance

Keep it responsible: sensible-staking language, age-appropriate framing, no
targeting of vulnerable users. When in doubt, dial the hype down.

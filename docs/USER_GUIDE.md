## 📊 Understanding the Table Headers:

### What the abbreviations mean:

| Header | Meaning | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **P** | Played (games) | 19 = 19 games played |
| **O8.5** | Over 8.5 corners | 75% = 75% of games had more than 8.5 corners |
| **O9.5** | Over 9.5 corners | 65% = 65% of games had more than 9.5 corners |
| **O10.5** | Over 10.5 corners | 50% = 50% of games had more than 10.5 corners |
| **U8.5** | Under 8.5 corners | 25% = 25% of games had less than 8.5 corners |
| **Avg** | Average per game | 9.2 = average 9.2 corners per game |

### For Goals:
- **O1.5** = Over 1.5 goals (2+ goals in the match)
- **O2.5** = Over 2.5 goals (3+ goals in the match)
- **O3.5** = Over 3.5 goals (4+ goals in the match)

### For Cards:
- **O2.5** = Over 2.5 cards (3+ cards in the match)
- **O3.5** = Over 3.5 cards (4+ cards in the match)
- **O4.5** = Over 4.5 cards (5+ cards in the match)

### For BTTS (Both Teams To Score):
- **BTTS Yes** = Both teams scored in the match
- **BTTS No** = At least one team didn't score
- **Avg GS** = Average goals scored
- **Avg GC** = Average goals conceded

## 🎨 Color Coding:

- 🟢 **Green (75%+)**: Very high probability - Strong bet
- 🟡 **Gold (60-74%)**: High probability - Good bet
- 🟠 **Orange (45-59%)**: Medium probability - Moderate bet
- 🔴 **Red (<45%)**: Low probability - Risky bet
